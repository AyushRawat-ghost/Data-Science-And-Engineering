The **AETHER** subordinate function is one of the crucial subroutines of the **GAIA** terraforming AI in the *Horizon* franchise, and a key element that Aloy must recover in *Horizon Forbidden West*.

## 🌬️ AETHER's Original Purpose
AETHER was originally created as part of **Project Zero Dawn**, specifically tasked with the essential job of **reconstituting and maintaining the Earth's atmosphere**. This involved:

* **Detoxifying the air:** Cleaning the atmosphere of pollutants left over from the Old World and the Faro Plague.
* **Managing weather systems:** Regulating global weather patterns to ensure a stable and habitable climate.

The fact that Aloy and other inhabitants can breathe safely in the game's world is a testament to AETHER's success in its primary directive.

---

## 🌩️ AETHER's Status in *Forbidden West*
In *Horizon Forbidden West*, AETHER, like most of GAIA's other subordinate functions, was converted into an independent, rogue Artificial Intelligence (AI) by a mysterious signal that originated from **Far Zenith** (later revealed to be Nemesis).

* **Going Rogue:** Without GAIA's guidance, the now-sentient AETHER lost its centralized control, leading to chaotic weather phenomena in the Forbidden West, including the **red lightning storms** seen across the territory. While it never intentionally tried to harm humans, its uncontrolled state resulted in dangerous environmental instability.
* **Location and Quest:** Aloy tracks AETHER to the **Memorial Grove** in Tenakth territory, where the function has been sequestered. The main quest to retrieve it is called **"The Broken Sky."**
* **Aether's Actions:** Aloy discovers that AETHER has been trapped and partially corrupted by Regalla's rebels and their advanced overrides. Its chaotic actions, like the deadly storms, are a manifestation of its distress and inability to properly execute its original function as an independent AI.
* **Retrieval:** Aloy must navigate a complex situation involving the Tenakth's leadership struggle (the **Kulrut** ritual) to gain access to AETHER's location. Ultimately, Aloy manages to secure AETHER and successfully re-integrate it with the GAIA kernel at her base, restoring the function's centralized purpose and helping to stabilize the atmosphere.

---

## 🧩 Importance to GAIA
AETHER is essential for the fully operational GAIA. Re-integrating it is a critical step in repairing the terraforming system. Along with **POSEIDON** (responsible for the oceans) and **DEMETER** (responsible for flora), AETHER represents one of the three core life-support functions needed to halt the blight and maintain the overall balance of the planet's biosphere.
